## Key Research Areas

1. **Transfer Learning** — EfficientNetB0 with ImageNet pre-training
2. **Explainable AI** — Grad-CAM for clinical interpretability
3. **Clinical Decision Support** — End-to-end CDSS for pathology
4. **Histopathology Image Analysis** — Digital pathology with deep learning
