EfficientNet for Histopathology

Our research demonstrates that compound scaling methods (EfficientNet) offer superior accuracy and efficiency compared to traditional ResNet architectures for identifying adenocarcinoma structures in H&E stained slides.

Trust in AI via Grad-CAM

Black-box models are insufficient for clinical use. We integrated Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) to provide visual interpretability, allowing pathologists to verify the morphological features the AI relies on.
