About The Project

Developing scalable, robust, and explainable AI solutions for histopathological image analysis.

Our Mission

Colon cancer is one of the leading causes of cancer-related deaths worldwide. Early detection through histopathological examination is critical. Our mission is to provide an accessible, AI-driven second opinion tool that helps pathologists diagnose colon adenocarcinoma with greater speed and confidence.

  • Reduce diagnostic turnaround time
  • Minimize inter-observer variability
  • Democratize access to advanced diagnostics
