Dataset Information

Trained on the highly regarded LC25000 Lung and Colon Histopathological Image Dataset.

Dataset Overview

The LC25000 dataset contains 25,000 color images (768 x 768 pixels) of lung and colon tissue patches. For the ColonSightAI project, we focused specifically on the colon tissue subset, which includes:

5,000

Colon Adenocarcinoma Images

5,000

Benign Colonic Tissue Images

Data augmentation techniques (random rotations, flips, and color jittering) were applied during training to improve model generalization.
